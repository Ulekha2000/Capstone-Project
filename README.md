**City-Parish Procurement Analytics (East Baton Rouge)**

An exploratory analysis of public procurement data, focusing on purchase orders and contracts across municipal departments in East Baton Rouge Parish, LA.

_**Why Select This Dataset?**_

**Real-World Complexity:** Uses actual municipal spending records with diverse department structures and complex transaction details.

**Public Expenditure Insights:** Offers clear visibility into local government spending across essential services like Police, EMS, Fleet Management, Libraries, and the Courts.

**Vendor & Location Dynamics:** Tracks spending across local versus out-of-parish vendors to see where taxpayer dollars go over time.

**Points to Find Out (Analytical Objectives)**__

**Departmental Spending Patterns:** Which City-Parish departments consume the largest share of the budget?

**Vendor Distribution:** How much business stays local versus going to external vendors?

**Transaction Types:** What is the balance between ongoing long-term service contracts and quick, direct purchase orders?

**Spending Trends:** Are there specific times of year or fiscal deadlines that trigger spikes in purchasing?

**Data Quality & Anomalies:** Where are the missing records, irregular purchase amounts, or unstandardized vendor names?

**Key Findings**_

**Top Budget Spenders:** Emergency and public safety services (Police, Fleet, EMS, and Sheriff-related departments) account for the highest frequency and dollar volume of purchases.

**Vendor Concentration:** Spending is heavily concentrated among key regional vendors, with recurring out-of-parish transactions reserved for specialized equipment and technical services.

**Data Gaps:** Historical logs show missing fields and non-standard entries, highlighting a clear need for better data-entry validation in local government systems.

**Analytical Steps Taken**

**Step 1 —** Data Ingestion: Loading raw purchasing logs, reviewing file formats, and inspecting initial record attributes.

**Step 2 —** Data Cleaning & Standardization: Standardizing department names, trimming clean text fields, and resolving missing values.

**Step 3 —** Exploratory Analysis: Aggregating totals per department, computing summary statistics, and mapping vendor locations.

**Step 4 —** Visualization: Generating spending breakdown charts, timeline trends, and vendor distribution maps.
